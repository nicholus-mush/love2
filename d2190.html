🌅 MORNING PRAYER

Awakening in His Grace • A New Day of Mercy
ADORATION – Praising God for a New Day
“O Lord, You are the God who spoke light out of darkness (Genesis 1:3). This is the day that You have made; I will rejoice and be glad in it (Psalm 118:24). Your mercies are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness (Lamentations 3:23). I awaken because You sustain me. Let every breath I take be a praise to You.”
📖 Lamentations 3:22-23 – “The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning.”
THANKSGIVING – Gratitude for Rest & Renewal
“Father, I thank You for the gift of sleep. You alone made me lie down in safety, and You have raised me up again. Thank You for the quiet hours of the night, for healing what was weary, for restoring my mind. Thank You for the warmth of my bed, for the roof over my head, and for the promise of this new beginning. I receive this morning as grace.”
📖 Psalm 3:5 – “I lie down and sleep; I wake again, because the Lord sustains me.”
CONFESSION – Surrendering Known & Unknown Sin
“Search me, O God, and know my heart this morning. Before this day begins, I confess that I cannot do this on my own. Forgive me for any bitterness I carried to sleep, for any worry that already grips my waking thoughts, for any plan I am making that does not honor You. Cleanse me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow (Psalm 51:7). Create in me a clean heart, O God (Psalm 51:10).”
📖 1 John 1:9 – “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.”
PETITION – Asking for Guidance & Strength
“Lead me this day, Lord. Do not let me walk in my own understanding, but in all my ways acknowledge You, that You may make my paths straight (Proverbs 3:5-6). For my work and duties: Give me wisdom, patience, and diligence. Let me work as for You and not for human praise (Colossians 3:23). For my relationships: Let no unwholesome word come from my mouth, but only what is helpful for building others up (Ephesians 4:29). Lead me not into temptation, but deliver me from evil (Matthew 6:13). When I am weak, let Your power be made perfect in me (2 Corinthians 12:9). Surround me with Your angels. Guard my going out today.”
📖 Psalm 121:8 – “The Lord will watch over your coming and going both now and forevermore.”
INTERCESSION – Praying for Others
“Lord, I lift up to You this morning: my family and loved ones – protect them, guide them, draw them close to You. My neighbours and colleagues – let me be a channel of Your peace. Those who have no rest this morning – the sick, the grieving, the homeless, the anxious. Send them comfort and help. Leaders and authorities – grant them wisdom and justice (1 Timothy 2:1-2).”
📖 Ephesians 6:18 – “Praying at all times in the Spirit, with all prayer and supplication.”
SURRENDER – Giving the Day to God
“I am not my own. I was bought with a price (1 Corinthians 6:19-20). So this day is not mine—it is Yours. My plans, my worries, my hopes, my fears—I lay them at Your feet. Not my will, but Yours be done (Luke 22:42). Whether I eat or drink, or whatever I do, let me do it all for Your glory (1 Corinthians 10:31). And if today brings suffering, give me grace. If today brings joy, give me gratitude.”
📖 Matthew 6:34 – “Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself.”
CLOSING – A Final Word of Faith
“I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me” (Philippians 4:13).

“Lord Jesus, go before me into this day. Walk beside me. Stay behind me to catch me if I fall. Let Your presence be more real to me than any problem I will face. I put my hand in Yours. Lead on.”
📖 Psalm 143:8 – “Let the morning bring me word of Your unfailing love, for I have put my trust in You.”
In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
