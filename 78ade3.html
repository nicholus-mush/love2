🌙 EVENING PRAYER

Rest in the Shadow of the Almighty • Peace for the Night
ADORATION – Praising God Who Never Sleeps
“O LORD, Lord of heaven and earth, You are the God who neither slumbers nor sleeps (Psalm 121:4). You are my refuge and my fortress, my God in whom I trust (Psalm 91:2). As the darkness falls, I lift my eyes to You, for my help comes from You alone. Holy, holy, holy is the Lord Almighty; the whole earth is full of Your glory (Isaiah 6:3).”
📖 Psalm 121:3-4 – “He who watches over Israel will neither slumber nor sleep.”
THANKSGIVING – Counting the Day’s Blessings
“Father, I thank You for this day – for breath, for strength, for bread on my table, and for the unseen ways You protected me. Thank You for moments of joy, for laughter shared, for work completed, and for grace to endure what was hard. ‘Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good; His love endures forever’ (Psalm 118:1). I receive this day as a gift from Your hand.”
📖 1 Thessalonians 5:18 – “Give thanks in all circumstances; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you.”
CONFESSION – Seeking Forgiveness and Cleansing
“Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts (Psalm 139:23). In silence, I bring before You my sins of this day:
— Forgive me for the words I spoke in haste…
— Forgive me for the love I withheld…
— Forgive me for the trust I placed in worry instead of in You…
‘If we confess our sins, You are faithful and just to forgive us and cleanse us from all unrighteousness’ (1 John 1:9). Lord Jesus, wash me in Your blood. Do not let any hidden fault rule over me, but make me clean.”
📖 Psalm 51:2 – “Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin!”
PETITION – Asking for Protection and Peace
“Grant me safe lodging this night. Place Your holy angels around my home (Psalm 34:7). Guard my mind from nightmares, my body from harm, and my heart from the fear of darkness.
‘I will both lay me down in peace and sleep, for You alone, O Lord, make me dwell in safety’ (Psalm 4:8).
Father, I also lift up those I love: (name them silently in your heart). Surround them with Your favor. For anyone in pain, sickness, or sorrow this night – be their comfort. And if I should not wake until the morning of resurrection, then let me wake in Your presence.”
📖 Psalm 3:5 – “I lie down and sleep; I wake again, because the Lord sustains me.”
SURRENDER – Resting in God’s Sovereignty
“Into Your hands I commit my spirit (Luke 23:46 – words of Jesus on the cross, and also Psalm 31:5). I cannot add a single hour to my life by worrying (Matthew 6:27), so I release tomorrow into Your care. Whatever comes – joy or trial – let me face it with Your peace. You are with me. Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me (Psalm 23:4).”
📖 Proverbs 3:24 – “When you lie down, you will not be afraid; when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.”
CLOSING – The Word of God as a Final Shield
“He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High will rest in the shadow of the Almighty” (Psalm 91:1).

“Lord Jesus, You are the Light of the World. As I close my eyes, keep me in Your light even in sleep. Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in Your sight, O Lord, my Rock and my Redeemer (Psalm 19:14).”
📖 Psalm 4:8 – “In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, Lord, make me dwell in safety.”
In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
🌙 WHISPER THIS AS YOU LAY DOWN: “Be at rest once more, O my soul, for the Lord has been good to you.” — Psalm 116:7
May the Lord bless your sleep and grant you His perfect peace.
“When you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.” — Proverbs 3:24
